# User interviews

The HackCanton Season 3 review named the lack of user conversations as a reason ShadowDesk missed the shortlist.
This file is the guide for those conversations and the log of what was heard.
Only record conversations that happened, with people who agreed to be recorded here, and keep names out unless they agree.

The current target is Canton node operators and app teams, as set out in [POSITIONING.md](POSITIONING.md).
The goal is five conversations before any more product work.

## Who to talk to

In order of priority:

1. People who run a Canton validator or super validator node, and decide or execute what happens to its Canton Coin income.
2. Canton app teams that earn application rewards in Canton Coin.
3. OTC desks or market makers active on Canton, who would answer the quote requests.
4. Node-as-a-service providers such as NODERS, who see many operators' setups.

## Where to find them

- The Canton Network forum (forum.canton.network): introductions, validator and operations threads.
- The Canton Network Discord: validator and developer channels.
- Other HackCanton teams that run their own validator: CompressRail, for example, runs its own DevNet validator.
- The NODERS team, through the HackCanton channels you already use.
- Public validator listings on Canton explorers.

## Messages

Send these yourself, adjusted to the person.
Keep the ask small: fifteen minutes, no pitch.

Direct message to a node operator:

> Hi, I'm building on Canton (ShadowDesk, from HackCanton Season 3). I'm trying to understand how node operators handle the Canton Coin they earn, for example converting part of it to dollars to cover costs. Would you have 15 minutes for a few questions? I'm not selling anything; I want to learn how it works today and where it hurts.

Forum or Discord post:

> Question for validator and app operators: how do you handle the Canton Coin you earn? Do you convert part of it to dollar stablecoins to cover costs, and if so, who does it, how often, and how do you limit what that person or bot can do? I'm researching this for a treasury tool and would value 15 minutes with anyone willing to share. Replies here or by DM both welcome.

Direct message to an OTC desk or market maker:

> Hi, I'm researching how Canton node operators convert Canton Coin into dollar stablecoins. If an operator sent your desk a sealed request for quote, visible only to you, would you answer it, and what would you need to see? Fifteen minutes would help a lot.

## Goal of each conversation

Learn whether operators convert Canton Coin regularly, who is trusted to do it, how that trust is limited, and whether a ledger-enforced mandate would change anything.
Do not pitch until the last five minutes.

## Questions for node operators and app teams

About the money today:

1. Roughly how much Canton Coin does your node or app earn in a month, and what share of it do you keep versus convert?
2. Walk me through the last time you converted Canton Coin to dollars or a stablecoin. Who decided, who did it, and where?
3. Who holds the keys that can move that Canton Coin? How many people or systems?
4. Is there a written limit on how much can be sold, at what price, or to whom? Who checks it?
5. Has a conversion ever gone wrong: too much sold, a bad price, the wrong counterparty, a key misused?

About automation:

6. Would you let software sell on a schedule for you? What would stop you?
7. If a second person had to approve a limit, and the network itself refused anything outside it, would that change your answer?

About price:

8. How do you get a price today? Do you ask more than one desk?
9. Would you want desks to quote without seeing each other's prices?

Closing:

10. What would have to be true before you tried this on a small amount?
11. Who else should I talk to?

## Questions for OTC desks and market makers

1. Do you quote Canton Coin today, and against which dollar tokens?
2. Would you answer a sealed request from a node operator that only you can see?
3. What do you need to know about the counterparty before quoting?
4. Would atomic settlement, where both sides move in one transaction or not at all, change your risk limits?
