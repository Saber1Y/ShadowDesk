# User interviews

The HackCanton Season 3 review named the lack of user conversations as a reason ShadowDesk missed the shortlist.
This file is the guide for those conversations and the log of what was heard.
Only record conversations that happened, with people who agreed to be recorded here, and keep names out unless they agree.

## Who to talk to

- Treasury operations staff at funds, corporates or crypto organisations that rebalance holdings.
- Risk officers who approve trading limits.
- Liquidity providers or OTC desks that answer RFQs.
- Canton node operators and app teams that convert Canton Coin revenue into dollar stablecoins.

## Goal of each conversation

Learn how limits on trading are set and enforced today, where that breaks, and whether a ledger-enforced mandate would change anything for them.
Do not pitch until the last five minutes.

## Questions

About the work today:

1. Walk me through the last time you rebalanced or converted a treasury holding. Who decided, who approved, who executed?
2. Where are the limits written down, and who or what checks them before a trade goes out?
3. Has a trade ever gone out that broke a limit? What happened, and how was it found?
4. How do you get prices for a large trade without moving the market?
5. How long does settlement take, and what can go wrong between agreeing a price and settling?

About automation:

6. Do you use, or plan to use, software or AI agents to execute trades? What stops you?
7. If an agent executed for you, what would you need to see before you trusted it?
8. Who would have to sign off on letting an agent trade?

About ShadowDesk (last five minutes):

9. If your risk officer's limits were enforced by the settlement network itself, so the agent physically could not exceed them, would that change your answer to question 6?
10. What is missing before you would try it on a small amount?
11. Who else should I talk to?
